# Academic template review

Reviewed Whimsical's template gallery and the supplied Research Design Canvas and Research Process boards on 22 October 2026. Recommendations below are adaptations for Zotero rather than claims that these formats automatically perform research.

| Format | Academic use | Current position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research Design | Connect a problem, literature, theory, questions, methodology, sample and contributions. | Added as a built-in format. |
| Research Review | Separate supported claims, challenges, emergent findings, open questions and implications. | Added as a built-in format. |
| Affinity Diagram | Organise source-linked observations into themes while retaining negative cases. | Added as a built-in format. |
| Debate Tree | Follow a proposition through evidence, objections, qualifications and replies. | Added as a built-in format. |
| Flowchart / decision tree | Show sampling decisions, analytical procedures and conditional reasoning. | Process Diagram and editable conditional chains provide a starting point. |
| Brainstorming / brainwriting | Develop research questions and possible explanations before evaluating them. | Useful future template using existing cards and groups; distinguish ideas from cited findings. |
| Swimlane | Separate participants, data sources or parallel stages in a research workflow. | Would benefit from labelled lanes and stable lane assignment. |
| Reading Kanban | Track To read, Annotating, Analysed and Used in writing. | Existing research Kanban objects provide a visual start; a dedicated version needs persistent task/status fields. |
| Assumption map / prioritisation matrix | Compare uncertainty and consequence of theoretical or methodological assumptions. | Useful future format; requires explicit axes and user-entered judgements. |
| Fishbone / root-cause map | Compare proposed mechanisms and rival explanations. | Useful template, with cited causal claims distinguished from hypotheses. |
| Timeline | Trace publication history or a concept's development. | Already available. |
| Gantt | Schedule reading, fieldwork, analysis and writing with dependencies. | Deferred to the next batch at the user's request. Needs real start/end dates, date-scaled bars, milestones, dependencies, validation and drag controls that change dates. |
| Wireframes, product briefs, business strategy and team icebreakers | Relevant for particular HCI or organisational projects, but less central to general Zotero research. | Lower priority than the source-focused formats. |

Source references: [Research Design Canvas](https://whimsical.com/adam-s-workspace9593/LEtdyo9JxRStT6U1sTM1U1), [Research Process](https://whimsical.com/adam-s-workspace9593/research-process-UvJaR4ciBttosqyryh17GQ).
